16 authors. 31 of the 180 works are given verse by verse.

The works of the Gauḍīya ācāryas, from Śrī Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana to Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, in the original language and in English, arranged work by work and searchable as one library.

Every verse is given in the original, in Bengali or Devanāgarī letters with its transliteration, followed by the commentaries of the ācāryas. Each verse also leads to the places where Śrīla Prabhupāda translates, explains or quotes it. Read the ācāryas in their own words and see that Śrīla Prabhupāda hands on their teaching faithfully.

Type with or without diacritics, for example anyabhilasita.

Read the first work: Upadeśāmṛta, text 1

Authors

16 authors, each with their works. A commentary stays with its book; an author’s page only points to it.

  • Śrīla Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa15 works

    Gauḍīya ācārya of Jaipur, disciple of Rādhā-dāmodara and student of Viśvanātha Cakravartī.

  • Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura17 works

    Son of Bhaktivinoda and founder of the Gauḍīya Maṭha.

  • Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura49 works

    Magistrate, writer and reformer who revived the Gauḍīya tradition in the nineteenth century.

  • Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu1 work

    Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu, who began the saṅkīrtana movement in Navadvīpa.

  • Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī20 works

    Nephew of Rūpa and Sanātana and the great systematiser of Gauḍīya theology.

  • Śrīla Kavi-karṇapūra6 works

    Paramānanda Sena, youngest son of Śivānanda Sena, blessed as a child by Śrī Caitanya at Purī.

  • Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī3 works

    Author of Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta, the biography and teachings of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, written in Vṛndāvana under the guidance of the Gosvāmīs.

  • Śrīla Locana dāsa Ṭhākura2 works

    Disciple of Narahari Sarakāra Ṭhākura of Śrīkhaṇḍa.

  • Śrīla Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura3 works

    Disciple of Lokanātha Gosvāmī and trained by Jīva Gosvāmī, he brought the teachings of the Gosvāmīs to Bengal and organised the festival at Kheturī.

  • Śrīla Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī11 works

    Sannyāsī from South India, uncle and teacher of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, who met Śrī Caitanya at Śrīraṅgam.

  • Śrīla Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī4 works

    Renounced great wealth to live with Śrī Caitanya at Purī under Svarūpa Dāmodara; later lived at Rādhā-kuṇḍa.

  • Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī17 works

    Chief of the six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana.


    First release: Upadeśāmṛta, text 1
  • Śrīla Sanātana Gosvāmī4 works

    Elder brother of Rūpa.

  • Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura13 works

    Ācārya in the line of Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura, author of the Sārārtha-darśinī commentaries on the Bhāgavatam and the Gītā.

  • Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura1 work

    Son of Nārāyaṇī and disciple of Nityānanda Prabhu, called the Vyāsa of Caitanya-līlā.

  • Śrīla Vyāsadeva14 works

    Compiler of the Vedic literature and author of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.
