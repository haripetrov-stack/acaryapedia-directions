The library, stage by stage

1 of 180 works released

The first release is the Upadēśamṛta: 11 texts with all their commentaries. Further works follow in stages, those most relevant to the followers of Śrīla Prabhupāda first. The order is still to be confirmed.

The planned library holds 180 works by 16 ācāryas; 31 of them are set out verse by verse.

Be told when a book comes out

One short message for each new book. This prototype sends nothing.
