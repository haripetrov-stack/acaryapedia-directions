The Nectar of Instruction, Text 1

Srila Prabhupada's translation and purport on Vanisource (NOI 1) His translation and purport are text of the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust. We link to them and do not reproduce them.

Śrīla Prabhupāda translated this text from Rūpa Gosvāmī’s Upadeśāmṛta. Behind his translation stand the verse itself, as it was printed in 1942, and the ācāryas who explained it.

Behind the book is the verse.

Rūpa Gosvāmī, Upadeśāmṛta, text 1, on a page of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition of 1942 (scan). The thread marks the four lines of the verse.

The verse, as printed and as set

Text 1 of Rūpa Gosvāmī’s Upadeśāmṛta in Bengali script, with its transliteration. The same lines, cropped from the 1942 page, stand beside them. Devanagari, translations and commentaries are on the verse page.

বাচো বেগং মনসঃ ক্রোধবেগংজিহ্বাবেগমুদরোপস্থবেগম্।এতান্ বেগান্ যো বিষহেত ধীরঃসর্ব্বামপীমাং পৃথিবীং সশিষ্যাৎ ॥১॥

vāco vegaṁ manasaḥ krodha-vegaṁjihvā-vegam udaropastha-vegametān vegān yo viṣaheta dhīraḥsarvām apīmāṁ pṛthivīṁ sa śiṣyāt
