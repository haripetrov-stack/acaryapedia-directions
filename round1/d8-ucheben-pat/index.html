What is in every lesson

  1. 1

    The verse

    In the original script and in IAST, with its translations. Each layer can be switched off.

  2. 2

    Key terms

    The words of the verse, each with a definition from the editors.

  3. 3

    Questions for reflection

    Written by the editors, for a student alone or a group together.

  4. 4

    Go deeper with Srila Prabhupada

    The books, lectures and conversations where he quotes the verse.

  5. 5

    Reading for the lesson

    The commentaries of the ācāryas.

Authors

In the first path

Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī

Chief of the six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana. Instructed by Śrī Caitanya at Prayāga (CC Madhya 19), he set out the theology of bhakti-rasa in Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu and Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi and wrote dramas, poems and hymns.

In this path: the 11 verses of the Upadeśāmṛta.

Śrīla Rādhāramaṇa dāsa Gosvāmī

Author of a Sanskrit commentary on the Upadeśāmṛta.

In this path: Upadeśa-prakāśikā-ṭīkā, in all 11 lessons.

Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura

Magistrate, writer and reformer who revived the Gauḍīya tradition in the nineteenth century: Jaiva-dharma, Hari-nāma-cintāmaṇi, Śaraṇāgati, commentaries and a large body of songs and essays.

In this path: Pīyūṣa-varṣiṇī-vṛtti in all 11 lessons and Bhāṣā in 8 lessons.

Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura

Son of Bhaktivinoda and founder of the Gauḍīya Maṭha. Author of the Anubhāṣya on Caitanya-caritāmṛta, lectures, essays and letters; spiritual master of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda.

In this path: Anuvṛtti in all 11 lessons and Padyānuvāda in 7 lessons.
