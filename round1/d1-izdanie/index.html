Every text of the Gauḍīya ācāryas, with its source in view

The works of the Gauḍīya ācāryas, from Śrī Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana to Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, in the original language and in English, arranged work by work and searchable as one library.

Every verse links to the places where Śrīla Prabhupāda translates, explains or quotes it.

Works with or without diacritics. Try upadesamrta or Rupa.

1 of 180 works is released: Upadeśāmṛta. The other 179 follow in stages. See the release plan

Śrīla Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa15 works

Gauḍīya ācārya of Jaipur, disciple of Rādhā-dāmodara and student of Viśvanātha Cakravartī. At Galta he defended the Gauḍīya line by writing the Govinda-bhāṣya on the Vedānta-sūtra; also the Gītā-bhūṣaṇa, Prameya-ratnāvalī, Siddhānta-ratna and commentaries on the Upaniṣads, the Bhāgavatam and the works of the Gosvāmīs.

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu1 work

Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu, who began the saṅkīrtana movement in Navadvīpa. He left only eight verses of instruction, the Śikṣāṣṭaka, given here with the commentaries of Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura.

  • Śrī Śikṣāṣṭaka

Śrīla Kavi-karṇapūra6 works

Paramānanda Sena, youngest son of Śivānanda Sena, blessed as a child by Śrī Caitanya at Purī. Author of Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā, Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū, Alaṅkāra-kaustubha and Caitanya-carita-mahākāvya.

Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī3 works

Author of Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta, the biography and teachings of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, written in Vṛndāvana under the guidance of the Gosvāmīs. Here with the two commentaries of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and Śrīla Prabhupāda's translation.

Śrīla Locana dāsa Ṭhākura2 works

Disciple of Narahari Sarakāra Ṭhākura of Śrīkhaṇḍa. Author of the Bengali Caitanya-maṅgala and of songs sung in Śrīla Prabhupāda's temples, such as Parama Koruṇā.

  • Śrī Caitanya-maṅgala
  • Dhāmālī

Śrīla Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura3 works

Disciple of Lokanātha Gosvāmī and trained by Jīva Gosvāmī, he brought the teachings of the Gosvāmīs to Bengal and organised the festival at Kheturī. His songs, Prārthanā and Prema-bhakti-candrikā, are quoted constantly by Śrīla Prabhupāda.

  • Prārthanā
  • Kuñja-varṇana
  • Śrī Prema-bhakti-candrikā

Śrīla Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī4 works

Renounced great wealth to live with Śrī Caitanya at Purī under Svarūpa Dāmodara; later lived at Rādhā-kuṇḍa. Author of Stavāvalī, Muktā-carita and Dāna-keli-cintāmaṇi.

Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī17 works

Chief of the six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana. Instructed by Śrī Caitanya at Prayāga (CC Madhya 19), he set out the theology of bhakti-rasa in Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu and Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi and wrote dramas, poems and hymns.

Śrīla Sanātana Gosvāmī4 works

Elder brother of Rūpa. Instructed by Śrī Caitanya at Vārāṇasī (CC Madhya 20–24). Author of Bṛhad-bhāgavatāmṛta; Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, the Gauḍīya manual of Vaiṣṇava practice, is traditionally attributed to him with Gopāla Bhaṭṭa.

  • Bṛhad-bhāgavatāmṛta
  • Hari-bhakti-vilāsa
  • Kṛṣṇa-līlā-stava
  • Bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī (on the Tenth Canto)

Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura13 works

Ācārya in the line of Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura, author of the Sārārtha-darśinī commentaries on the Bhāgavatam and the Gītā. Here his Stavāmṛta-laharī, whose first hymn, the Gurv-aṣṭaka, is sung every morning in Śrīla Prabhupāda's temples, and his Mādhurya-kādambinī on the stages of bhakti, both in Bhanu Swami's English.

Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura1 work

Son of Nārāyaṇī and disciple of Nityānanda Prabhu, called the Vyāsa of Caitanya-līlā. His Caitanya-bhāgavata is the first full biography of Śrī Caitanya, given here with Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura's Gauḍīya-bhāṣya in English.

  • Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata

Release plan

  1. Opening release Upadeśāmṛta

    11 texts with all their commentaries, released when acaryapedia.org opens.

  2. Next works
