# Acaryapedia: eight design directions

Prototypes for choosing the look and the reading model of Acaryapedia. Round 1 of 2.

**Open the comparison page:** https://haripetrov-stack.github.io/acaryapedia-directions/

Directions 2 to 9 show the same real text, Upadeśāmṛta, text 1, by Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī: a home page and a verse page, on a phone and on a computer. Variant 1 is the first prototype of 23 September 2026, a verse page for Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.1.11, with every translation under copyright withheld. Open the pages, pick a first, second and third choice, add a note, and send it to Pitambara (the page has a button that copies your choice). Pitambara and Mahendra Prabhu choose three finalists; the direction is then decided together with Visnu Murti Prabhu and Kasya Prabhu.

About these prototypes:

- They use only the free layers: the Sanskrit and Bengali originals, the IAST transliteration and the Bengali commentaries of the 1942 Gauḍīya Maṭha edition. Śrīla Prabhupāda's translation and purport appear only as a link to Vanisource. English commentaries still waiting for permission are marked placeholders.
- Links to other authors and works lead nowhere yet.
- The pages carry `noindex`, so search engines leave them out; the real site will be acaryapedia.org.
- The prototypes are for choosing and will be replaced; please do not link to them from other sites.
