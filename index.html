Eight ways to read Acaryapedia

Each direction is a different way into the library, with its own look. All eight show the same real text, Upadeśāmṛta, text 1, by Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī, so the comparison is fair. Variant 1 is the first prototype of 23 September 2026 (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.1.11, a verse page only). Today's working version is the last panel, for reference.

Open the pages, then pick your first, second and third choice and add a note. Pitambara and Mahendra Prabhu choose three finalists; the direction is then decided together with Visnu Murti Prabhu and Kasya Prabhu.

About these prototypes

    
    
      
  • They use only the free layers: the Sanskrit and Bengali originals, the IAST transliteration and the Bengali commentaries of the 1942 Gauḍīya Maṭha edition. Śrīla Prabhupāda's translation and purport appear only as a link to Vanisource until there is permission from the BBT. English commentaries still waiting for permission are shown as marked placeholders.
    • 
      
  • Every page was checked on a phone width of 375 pixels and a computer width of 1280: nothing runs off the screen, every verse page stays under 300 KB, and the text and layers can be read without JavaScript.
    • 
      
  • They were checked in Chromium only, not yet in Safari, Firefox or on a real phone.
    • 
      
  • Links to other authors and works lead nowhere yet. Some details are proposals that need the editorial team, for example the order of the stations in direction 6 and the lessons that come after Upadeśāmṛta in direction 9.
